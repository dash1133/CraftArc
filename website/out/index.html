Key insights

The firm is building depth at Principal and MD level. The real risk is the Partner layer: behind on the new competencies, slow to be acted on, and short of the development that works best.

How far is each level from target?

The gap has moved to the top.

56% of Partners are below target, against 18% of Principals and 0% of MDs. No Partner is ready for the next level.

Is the bench getting deeper, and what happened to last year's below-target group?

The bench is getting deeper.

80% of the firm is at or above target, up 16 points in a year. Raising the targets on the emerging competencies in FY24 pushed the below-target share up for a while.

Where are we weakest?

The new muscle is the weak muscle.

29% to 33% are below target on the emerging competencies, against at most 4% on any traditional one. Only 28% of Partners are at or above target on them.

Are we acting on people who are not making it?

Managing out is slow.

5 people have been below target for 3 years or more, and 3 of them have no action recorded. The median time to a decision is 32 months.

Is development time going where it works?

Development time is in the wrong place.

Mentoring and non-traditional development go with the most growth, yet get 29% of hours. People who used all four levers grew about six times as fast as those who used one or none.

Built on five measurement rules. Measure the size of the gap as well as the count, and count each person once. Judge exits by their quality, follow each group over time, and look at how development levers combine.
