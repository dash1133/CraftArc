Leadership Cockpit

Not yet live

Who uses it The CEO, the leadership team and the board.

What the agent will do Keep the slate of successors your leadership team sets for each plant manager and operations role. Each slate shows who is ready now, ready in 3 years and ready in 5 to 7 years.

Review Cycle

Who uses it Each leader, the colleagues who give feedback and the evaluation coordinator who runs the review.

What the agent does Runs each self-evaluation and each 360-degree dialogue, then drafts the write-ups against your own standard for each level.

Development Arc

Who uses it Each leader and their evaluation coordinator.

What the agent does Turns the write-ups into development commitments, and helps each leader track them through the year.

The CraftArc agent runs the Review Cycle and the Development Arc, and will run the Leadership Cockpit. It interviews, grades the evidence by source and drafts the write-ups. The evaluation coordinator owns the final wording.
