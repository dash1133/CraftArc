The evidence

Judgment lives in people, so the funds that last are the ones that grow them

About 4x

One study followed nearly 4,000 buyout managers at about 800 firms. The individual mattered about four times as much as the firm in explaining returns.

Braun, Dorau, Jenkinson and Urban, working paper, covering 1970 to 2017. Reported by Institutional Investor.

Partners leave

When senior partners leave, raising the next fund gets harder.

Ivashina and Lerner, "Pay now or pay later? The economics within the private equity partnership", Journal of Financial Economics, 2019. Working paper at NBER.

Leadership is what turns a fund into an institution.

The problem

The craft is rarely written down, and the arc is rarely traced

  • Most private equity firms have never written down their craft: what it takes to lead an investment fund, competency by competency.
  • Few help each leader trace their arc, the progress they make on every competency their craft demands.
  • So the craft stays in a few heads, and feedback arrives once a year, too late to use.

How it works

Five conversations, one arc

The agent is an expert in conversation. Here is how a review cycle unfolds. The agent lines below are sample dialogue.

  1. Self-evaluation
  2. Design the feedback
  3. Contributor interviews
  4. Prepare the dialogue
  5. Development plan

Conversation 1

Self-evaluation

The agent talks with each person for their self-evaluation, by voice or text, in about 25 minutes. It maps each answer to your competencies and asks for whatever is missing.

CraftArc AgentTo get started, what have you worked on over the period?

Conversation 2

Design the feedback

It helps the evaluation coordinator design the feedback interviews, so the right people speak to each competency. Before the survey is complete, it flags the gaps.

CraftArc AgentNobody who saw the banker meetings is on the list yet. Shall we add someone who did?

Conversation 3

Contributor interviews

It speaks with each contributor and gathers what they saw first-hand in one place. Every fact is graded as directly observed, hearsay or third party.

CraftArc AgentTell me about a call you saw Arjun make on Atlas. What did you see first-hand?

Conversation 4

Prepare the dialogue

It drafts the memo for your Executive Committee, a note to the person and a scorecard. The evaluation coordinator owns the final wording.

CraftArc AgentI'm drafting the review documents now. I'll let you know as each one is ready.

Conversation 5

Development plan

It helps each person build their own development plan, with goals drawn from the evaluation coordinator's write-up, and track it through the year.

CraftArc AgentWhich of these goals would you like to start with?

FAQ

Questions partners ask us

Who decides where someone sits on each competency?

Your evaluation coordinator does. The interviews gather graded facts, and the coordinator forms the view with the agent, which maps each example to the competencies in your framework.

We use our own roles, competencies and steps. Will it fit?

Everything specific to your firm is written in plain English, including roles, competencies and steps, so CraftArc adapts to it without code.

Why not build this with a general AI assistant?

CraftArc holds your framework in plain English, and it gives every role one home and five tracked steps. It grades every fact by source and checks coverage before you complete the survey. One firm tried the do-it-yourself route first, as the story above shows.

Will our people take part?

The coach is conversational and never nags. If someone does not want to answer, it asks once or twice and then lets them go.

How long is each conversation?

Each conversation with the coach takes about 25 minutes, by voice or text. Partners who coordinate reviews or give contributor interviews will have more than one.

Will it disrupt the review process we already run?

You start with your senior deal team only, which keeps the disruption low.

Who sees my self-evaluation?

Only you and your evaluation coordinator.

Are contributor interviews anonymous?

Yes, if your firm wants them to be. Your firm chooses how, and the transcripts are stored securely.

Who runs it inside the firm?

We recommend an internal business champion who owns the cycle. An evaluation coordinator runs each person's review, while the agent interviews, grades the evidence and drafts the write-ups.

What does it cost?

Participants and evaluation coordinators are unlimited, and contributors are never charged. We quote the fee after a short call about your team and your framework.
