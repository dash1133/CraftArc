Board view

Key insightsThe firm is building depth at Principal and MD level. The real risk is the Partner layer: behind on the new competencies, slow to be acted on, and short of the development that works best.

Gap to Target

QuestionHow far is each level from target?

The gap has moved to the top. 56% of Partners are below target, against 18% of Principals and 0% of MDs. No Partner is ready for the next level.

Trajectory

QuestionIs the bench getting deeper, and what happened to last year's below-target group?

The bench is getting deeper. 80% of the firm is at or above target, up 16 points in a year. Raising the targets on the emerging competencies in FY24 pushed the below-target share up for a while.

Competencies in Focus

QuestionWhere are we weakest?

The new muscle is the weak muscle. 29% to 33% are below target on the emerging competencies, against at most 4% on any traditional one. Only 28% of Partners are at or above target on them.

Managing Out

QuestionAre we acting on people who are not making it?

Managing out is slow. 5 people have been below target for 3 years or more, and 3 of them have no action recorded. The median time to a decision is 32 months.

Development Efforts

QuestionIs development time going where it works?

Development time is in the wrong place. Mentoring and non-traditional development go with the most growth, yet get 29% of hours. People who used all four levers grew about six times as fast as those who used one or none.

7Trust

Trust

Built for trust, not automation.

People make every call. The agent gathers evidence and drafts. Evaluators and boards decide.

Every fact is traceable. Every assessment links back to its source and its evidence tag.

Designed for candor. Colleagues can give feedback anonymously. Leadership gets a frank version, and each leader gets a constructive one.

Feeds your existing HR stack. A screwdriver, not a Swiss Army knife. CraftArc feeds Workday or your HRIS rather than replacing it.
